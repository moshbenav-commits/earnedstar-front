# How to reset your EarnedStar password

If you've forgotten your EarnedStar password, you can reset it yourself from
the sign-in page — no need to contact support.

1. Click **"Forgot password?"** below the password field on the sign-in page.
   This opens the password reset request page.
2. Enter the email address on your account and click **"Send reset link"**.
3. Open the reset email and click the link inside it. The page shows
   **"Check your email"** either way, so it never confirms or denies whether
   an account exists for that address.
4. Enter a new password and confirm it on the **"Set a new password"**
   screen. Passwords must be at least 8 characters.
5. Click **"Update password"** to save it.
6. Wait for the **"Password updated"** message. You're redirected to the
   sign-in page automatically, where you can sign in with your new password.

# How to cancel or change your EarnedStar subscription

There isn't a self-serve "Cancel Subscription" button in the dashboard yet —
cancelling goes through support, but you can check your plan and export your
data yourself first.

1. Open the **pricing page** to review your current plan and billing terms.
   Plans are billed via Authorize.net, with a 14-day free trial and
   cancellation anytime.
2. Read **"What happens to my reviews if I cancel?"** in the FAQ section on
   the pricing page. Your reviews stay yours — you can export them at any
   time, and you get a 30-day window after cancelling to download
   everything.
3. Sign in and open the **Settings** page in your dashboard to confirm your
   current plan. A Plan badge near the top of Settings shows what you're on.
4. On the **Reviews** page in your dashboard, click **"Export CSV"** to save
   a copy of your review data before cancelling.
5. Email **support@earnedstar.com** to request the cancellation or plan
   change. Self-serve cancellation isn't available in the dashboard yet —
   support makes the change and confirms once it's done.

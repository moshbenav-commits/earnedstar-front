# How to create your EarnedStar account and start your free trial

EarnedStar doesn't have an appointment-booking flow — the closest real task
on the site is creating your merchant account, which starts your 14-day free
trial immediately.

1. Go to the sign-up page. It opens directly on the **"Create Account"**
   form — there's no separate tab to click first.
2. Fill in your business name, email, and a password. A strength meter below
   the password field shows whether it's Weak, Okay, or Strong.
3. Confirm your password in the second password field.
4. Check the box agreeing to the **Terms & Privacy Policy**.
5. Click **"Create Account — 14-day trial"**.
6. Wait to be redirected into your new EarnedStar dashboard — the redirect
   confirms your account and trial were created successfully.
